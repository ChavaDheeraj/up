# Phase 3 Quantitative Survey Instrument & SEM Model Specification
**Title:** Assessing Perceived Safety of Solo Women Travellers
**Bridge:** Operationalized from Braun & Clarke (2006) Thematic Analysis of Travel Blogs & Semi-Structured Interviews.

## Conceptual Structural Equation Model (SEM) Hypotheses
| Hypothesis | Structural Pathway | Expected Sign | Theoretical Formulation |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **H1** | `Physical Constraints -> Perceived Safety` | negative | Higher perceived physical constraints (harassment, transit vulnerabilities, lodging insecurity) significantly reduce perceived safety among solo women travellers. |
| **H2** | `Social Constraints -> Perceived Safety` | negative | Higher perceived social constraints (patriarchal norms, family anxiety, gender discrimination) negatively affect perceived safety. |
| **H3** | `Constraint Negotiation -> Perceived Safety` | positive (mediating/buffering) | Active deployment of constraint negotiation strategies (behavioral adaptation, situational awareness, digital tracking) mitigates constraint impact and elevates perceived safety. |
| **H4** | `Perceived Safety -> Destination Image` | positive | Higher perceived safety positively enhances the cognitive and affective destination image among solo women travellers. |
| **H5** | `Destination Image -> Travel Intention` | positive | A favorable and safe destination image strongly predicts solo female travel intention. |
| **H6** | `Perceived Safety -> Well-Being & Self-Leadership` | positive | Enhanced perceived safety during travel fosters greater psychological well-being, empowerment, and self-leadership. |

## Operationalized Measurement Scales (N = 400 Women Travellers)
| Construct | Item Code | Survey Indicator Statement | Scale |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| Physical Constraints | **PC1** | "I worry about being followed, catcalled, or stared at when navigating public spaces alone." | 7-point Likert (1=Strongly Disagree, 7=Strongly Agree) |
| Physical Constraints | **PC2** | "I perceive nocturnal transit (e.g., night trains, deserted bus stops, unlicensed taxis) as high-risk for solo female travel." | 7-point Likert |
| Physical Constraints | **PC3** | "I am concerned about accommodation vulnerabilities (e.g., weak door locks, ground floor rooms, unsecured dorms)." | 7-point Likert |
| Social Constraints | **SC1** | "In many destinations, I feel constrained by patriarchal norms and restrictive cultural expectations for women." | 7-point Likert |
| Social Constraints | **SC2** | "My family and social circle express persistent anxiety and protective pressure regarding my solo travel." | 7-point Likert |
| Social Constraints | **SC3** | "I worry about societal victim-blaming or judgment if a safety incident occurs while travelling unaccompanied." | 7-point Likert |
| Constraint Negotiation | **CN1** | "I deliberately alter my presentation (e.g., purposeful walking, fake rings, avoiding eye contact) to deter unwanted attention." | 7-point Likert |
| Constraint Negotiation | **CN2** | "I use digital safety tools (e.g., live location tracking, fake phone calls, offline maps) to maintain virtual security tethers." | 7-point Likert |
| Constraint Negotiation | **CN3** | "I strictly plan my movements around daylight hours and vet accommodation reviews specifically written by women." | 7-point Likert |
| Constraint Negotiation | **CN4** | "I rely on embodied intuition and gut instincts to exit situations as soon as something feels off." | 7-point Likert |
| Perceived Safety | **PS1** | "During my solo journeys, I experience an overall sense of security and psychological comfort." | 7-point Likert |
| Perceived Safety | **PS2** | "My subjective feeling of safety is shaped more by my situational vigilance than by sensationalized media reports." | 7-point Likert |
| Perceived Safety | **PS3** | "I frequently feel vulnerable or apprehensive when walking alone in unfamiliar travel environments. (R)" | 7-point Likert (Reverse Coded) |
| Destination Image | **DI1** | "A destination's perceived safety reputation is a primary criterion in my positive evaluation of that country/city." | 7-point Likert |
| Destination Image | **DI2** | "I view destinations with welcoming, respectful host communities as female-friendly travel destinations." | 7-point Likert |
| Destination Image | **DI3** | "I have confidence that local authorities and emergency services in the destination would protect a solo female traveller." | 7-point Likert |
| Travel Intention | **TI1** | "Perceived safety concerns directly influence whether I visit, alter the itinerary of, or boycott a destination." | 7-point Likert |
| Travel Intention | **TI2** | "I intend to undertake solo travel adventures in the next 12 to 24 months." | 7-point Likert |
| Travel Intention | **TI3** | "I actively encourage and advocate for other women to experience solo travel." | 7-point Likert |
| Well-Being & Self-Leadership | **WB1** | "Solo travel has significantly elevated my self-reliance, problem-solving capability, and self-leadership." | 7-point Likert |
| Well-Being & Self-Leadership | **WB2** | "Successfully negotiating safety challenges and overcoming fear gives me a profound sense of empowerment." | 7-point Likert |
| Well-Being & Self-Leadership | **WB3** | "Solo travel enhances my psychological well-being, mindfulness, and personal fulfillment." | 7-point Likert |
