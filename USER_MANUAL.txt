1. INTRODUCTION & RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Welcome to the Qualitative Research Analysis Workspace. This system is designed 
specifically to assist academic researchers in conducting rigorous qualitative 
thematic analysis on data regarding "Perceived Safety of Solo Women Travellers".

Core Operational Principle:
    "The AI proposes. The evidence supports. The researcher validates."

The system strictly enforces Braun & Clarke's (2006) six-stage methodology:
  Stage 1: Familiarisation with the data
  Stage 2: Generating initial codes
  Stage 3: Searching for themes
  Stage 4: Reviewing themes
  Stage 5: Defining and naming themes
  Stage 6: Producing the report

Dataset Architecture:
  - Cohort A (Blogs): ~40 publicly available solo-travel blog posts written by women.
  - Cohort B (Interviews): ~50 semi-structured interview transcripts with international women students.
  - Phase 3 (Survey): Quantitative SEM questionnaire operationalized from qualitative themes.


2. SYSTEM QUICK-START & ACCESS

4. SECTION-BY-SECTION USER GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 OVERVIEW DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Provides a real-time summary of project progress, corpus statistics, and methodology pipeline.
- How to Use:
  1. Review Top Stat Cards: Check Total Documents (40 Blogs / 50 Interviews), Words Analyzed, 
     Initial Codes Generated, and Final Themes.
  2. Inspect the Stepper: Monitor the 8-step pipeline indicator (Ingestion -> Familiarisation -> 
     Initial Coding -> Codebook -> Themes -> Review & Compare -> Framework & Validate -> Report).
  3. Analyze Construct Frequency Distribution: View horizontal bars displaying the frequency 
     of the 7 theoretical constructs across all coded text.

4.2 UPLOAD DATA (INGESTION & DIRECT OUTPUT TABLES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Upload raw research documents and immediately view full qualitative analysis output tables.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Click on the "Upload Data" sidebar item.
  2. Drag & Drop your research file (PDF, DOCX, TXT, HTML, or CSV) onto the upload drop zone, 
     or click to browse your local computer.
  3. Select Dataset Classification: Choose "Public Travel Blog Post (40 Cohort)" or 
     "Semi-structured Interview Transcript (50 Cohort)".
  4. Click "Process & Code Document". The live progress bar will track parsing, segmentation, 
     and hybrid ML coding.
  5. Review Direct Output Tables (Rendered directly under Ingestion & Coding Results):
     - Table A (Document Metrics): Filename, Doc ID, Title, Type, Word Count, Paragraph Count, Density %, Dominant Construct.
     - Table B (Construct Frequency Breakdown): Construct Name, Segment Frequency, Percentage, Definition.
     - Table C (Key Evidence Quotes): Primary Construct, Sub-dimension, Verbatim Quote, Confidence Score, Emotional Tone.
  6. Click "Download Full Document Excel Report" to export a standalone .xlsx for this document.

4.3 DOCUMENTS REPOSITORY & FAMILIARISATION SUMMARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Manage the study corpus and complete Stage 1 (Familiarisation) of Braun & Clarke.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Use the Search Bar to search for documents by ID (e.g., BLOG_01, INT_04) or Title.
  2. Use Filters to narrow down by Dataset Type (Blogs Only / Interviews Only) or Status.
  3. Click the "Familiarisation" button on any row.
  4. In the pop-up drawer, review structured insights:
     - Main Experience
     - Destination & Traveller Context
     - Key Safety Concerns & Emotional Responses
     - Positive & Negative Lived Experiences

4.4 INITIAL CODING & EVIDENCE TRACEABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Inspect passage-level qualitative coding and verify exact document origin.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Filter by Construct (e.g., Physical Constraints, Perceived Safety, Well-Being) or source type.
  2. Use the keyword search box to search for specific terms (e.g., "night transport", "fake ring").
  3. Examine each Coded Card:
     - Read verbatim quotation.
     - Check Document Traceability: Source Document ID (e.g., BLOG_01) and Paragraph Number (e.g., Paragraph #19).
     - Inspect ML Metadata: Confidence score, emotional tone, and matched indicator keywords.

4.5 CODEBOOK MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Review, refine, approve, or reject qualitative codes generated by the model.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Scroll through the Dynamic Codebook table.
  2. Inspect fields: Code Name, Theoretical Definition, Verbatim Evidence Sample, Construct, 
     Frequency, Document Reach, Confidence.
  3. Approve or Reject: Click "Approve" to validate a code for inclusion in theme clustering, 
     or click "Reject" to exclude it.

4.6 CANDIDATE THEMES & SUB-THEMES REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Group initial codes into broader theoretical themes and execute Stage 4 & 5 review.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Review Theme Cards: Examine Theme Name, Definition, Subthemes, Related Codes, 
     Blog Frequency, Interview Frequency, and Verbatim Evidence Count.
  2. Approve Theme: Click "Approve Theme" to confirm candidate theme into final findings.
  3. Reject Theme: Click "Reject" to discard a weak or redundant theme.
  4. Rename Theme: Click "Rename", enter your custom researcher title, and submit.

4.7 BLOG VS INTERVIEW COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Compare public voluntary narratives against direct personal interview transcripts.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Read the Comparative Synthesis summary box.
  2. Examine the Cross-Dataset Table:
     - Compare presence in Blog Cohort (Public) vs Interview Cohort (Personal).
     - Check Tag: Convergence (present in both), Divergence (stronger in interviews), 
       Blog-Specific, or Interview-Specific.
     - Read Evidence Notes detailing contextual differences.

4.8 CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK MAPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Map qualitative evidence to the theoretical model:
  Physical & Social Constraints -> Perceived Safety -> Destination Image -> Travel Intention -> Well-Being
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Review Conceptual Model statement.
  2. Inspect Framework Cards showing Theme -> Construct -> Verbatim Quote -> Document ID & Paragraph #.
  3. Check Evidence Level Tag:
     - Direct Evidence (explicit participant statement)
     - Reasonable Interpretation (analytical inference)
     - Theoretical Interpretation (broader theoretical link)

4.9 VALIDATION AUDIT & METRIC TRACKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Maintain complete transparency and calculate researcher-model validation metrics.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Inspect Metric Stat Boxes: Agreement Rate %, Precision, Recall, and F1 Score.
  2. Scroll through the Audit Log table to view a full timestamped history of all 
     researcher approvals, rejections, and modifications.

4.10 BLIND MODEL TESTING WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Run independent reliability tests to evaluate model coding quality on blind samples.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Click "Run Blind Evaluation".
  2. The system runs independent coding across the 9 target blog documents without showing labels.
  3. Review output metrics: Documents Tested (9), Overall Agreement Rate %, Macro Precision, 
     Macro Recall, and Macro F1 Score.
  4. Inspect Disagreement Analysis: Review passages where model predictions differed from 
     ground-truth labels to identify edge cases for researcher overrides.

4.11 EXPORT & ACADEMIC REPORTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Download export packages for academic publication and CAQDAS integration.
- Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Excel Master Codebook: Click "Download Excel Codebook" to get a multi-tab workbook (.xlsx).
  2. NVivo XML: Click "Download NVivo XML" to export QSR NVivo compatible coding structures.
  3. Phase 3 Survey Items: Click "Download Survey Items (CSV)" to get 7-point Likert scale items.
  4. Full Thematic Analysis Report: Click "Download Full Report (.md)" to export the complete report.


5. OPERATIONAL BEST PRACTICES & TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Document Traceability: Never delete raw files once ingested; the database relies on 
  Document IDs (BLOG_01, INT_01) to preserve verbatim quotation links.
- Researcher Authority: If the model assigns a code or theme with low confidence, use 
  the Codebook or Themes module to override the decision.
- Vercel Deployment: The system is pre-configured for Vercel with serverless fallback 
  to `/tmp/research.db` for instant hosting.
